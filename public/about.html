VTasker

Cross-country task planning for paraglider and hang glider pilots

VTasker is a free web app for planning cross-country competition tasks, sharing them with other pilots, agreeing a day to fly them, and scoring the flights afterwards.

It is run by Hamish Dicker for the New Zealand free-flying community, and is used alongside Nelson & Bays Paragliding.

What it does

Plan a task

Draw a route on the map and VTasker builds a competition task from it — start cylinder, turnpoints, end of speed section and goal — snapping to official waypoints where you have them loaded.

Send it to your instrument

Every task exports as an XCTrack QR code, so pilots can scan it straight into their flight instrument at launch.

Share and vote

Publish a task for others to browse on a map, vote on, and copy into their own planner.

Agree a fly day

Schedule a day to fly a task. Pilots confirm they are competing, and can propose and vote on a change to the start time.

Score the flights

Upload your IGC flight log and VTasker checks it against the task cylinders, working out your distance, speed section time and points.

Pilot rankings (beta)

A chess-style rating that rewards beating higher-rated pilots more than lower-rated ones, built from results across fly days.

Why you sign in

Planning a task needs no account — you can open VTasker, draw a task and export it to your instrument without signing in at all.

An account is only needed for the parts that involve other people: publishing a task, voting, joining a fly day, and submitting a flight for scoring. Signing in with Google shares only your name, profile picture and email address with VTasker, and nothing else from your Google account. Email sign-in without Google is also offered.

What is stored, who can see it, and how to have it deleted is set out in the privacy policy.

Scoring

VTasker uses a simplified scoring scheme inspired by CIVL GAP: points for distance flown, points for speed for pilots who complete the speed section, and a bonus for reaching goal, each measured against the best pilot on the day. It is deliberately not a CIVL-compliant scorer and is not used for official competition results.
