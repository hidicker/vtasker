VTasker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

VTasker is a free tool for planning, sharing and scoring cross-country paragliding and hang gliding tasks. This page explains what it stores about you, who can see it, and how to get it removed.

VTasker is operated by Hamish Dicker ("we", "us"). You can reach us at hidicker@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You can browse and build tasks without an account. We only store anything about you once you sign in.
  • Signing in stores your email address and a display name — nothing else about your identity.
  • Tasks you publish, votes you cast, fly days you join, your results and your rating are visible to other people. That is the point of the app.
  • Flight logs contain a detailed record of where you flew. Only signed-in pilots can download them.
  • We do not sell your data, run advertising, or use tracking or analytics cookies.
  • Ask us and we will delete your account and everything attached to it.

About flight logs

An IGC file is a second-by-second GPS record of an entire flight. It can show where you launched, the route you flew, and where you landed — which may be near your home or other places you would rather not publish.

Uploads are stored privately and are not published on the open web, but any signed-in pilot can download them. This is deliberate, so results can be checked. Only upload flights you are comfortable sharing with other users of the site.

If you sign in with Google

We ask Google only for your name, profile picture and email address. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, calendar or anything else. Google tells you exactly what is being shared before you agree. You can revoke this at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

What we do not collect

No advertising or analytics cookies, no tracking pixels, no browsing history and no payment details. The only cookie-equivalent we use is the sign-in token that keeps you logged in, stored by your browser.

The app may ask your browser for your current location, so it can centre the map where you are. That position is used on your device only — it is never sent to us, stored, or shared, and declining the permission simply leaves the map where it is. The one exception is the flight logs you choose to upload, which do contain location data and are covered above.

Where your data is stored

Accounts, tasks, results and flight logs are stored with Supabase, on servers in Singapore. This means your information is held outside New Zealand. Supabase processes it on our instructions and does not use it for its own purposes. See the Supabase privacy policy.

Other services your browser contacts

VTasker draws maps and loads a few resources from third parties. When it does, those providers can see your IP address and which map tiles you requested. We do not send them your name, email or flight data.

  • Map tiles: OpenStreetMap, OpenTopoMap, and Esri (satellite imagery)
  • Thermal and skyway overlays: thermal.kk7.ch
  • Site resources: Cloudflare (cdnjs), unpkg, and the Tailwind CSS CDN
  • Google: only if you choose to sign in with Google

How long we keep it

  • Your account — until you ask us to delete it.
  • Flight logs — kept so results stay checkable. Ask us and we will delete yours.
  • Published tasks, results and ratings — these form part of a shared record. If you delete your account we will remove your name from them or delete them, as you prefer.

Your rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 you can ask us to:

  • See what we hold about you
  • Correct anything wrong — you can change your display name yourself in the app at any time
  • Delete your account and associated data

Email hidicker@gmail.com and we will action it within 20 working days. If you are unhappy with how we handle a privacy issue, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Sign-in uses either a one-time email link or Google — VTasker never sees or stores a password. Database access rules restrict each pilot to changing only their own votes, participation and uploads. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not upload anything you would be seriously harmed by disclosing.

Children

VTasker is intended for pilots and is not designed for children under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change how data is used in a material way, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
